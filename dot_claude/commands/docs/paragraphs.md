---
description: Split long paragraphs into short ones, one idea each, in pasted prose or in the files you name.
argument-hint: <prose | file...>
disable-model-invocation: true
---

Input: $ARGUMENTS

- Prose: split the paragraphs over 6 sentences, and return the result.
- File paths: split the paragraphs over 6 sentences in those files. Do not commit.

How to split:

- Insert a paragraph break where the topic, step, or subject changes, so each paragraph holds one idea.
- Turn a sequence of steps into a numbered list, and a comparison of items on shared attributes into a table.
- Rewrite a sentence only where the split needs it, for example to cut a run-on sentence in two or to drop a connector that no longer fits.
- Keep the technical meaning and every fact, and the markup of the file.
