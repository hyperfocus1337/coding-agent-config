- Name the failure mode first: what breaks, and under which input or condition.
- Name the cost: the work, the dependency, and the code somebody has to maintain.
- Give the cheaper alternative, or say that none exists.
- Agree after this, and say what changed your mind.
- Do not invent a problem to fill the list. Say "I found no problem" when you found none.
